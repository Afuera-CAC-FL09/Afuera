## 2. Athletic trainer: WBGT meter (Oct 15)

**Subject:** Borrow your WBGT meter for an hour on Oct 17 or 18?

Hi [Name],

My team is building a student app that estimates WBGT from the weather forecast, using the Liljegren model and the FHSAA Policy 41.8.3 levels. Before we submit it, we want to check it against a real meter.

Could we borrow (or watch you use) the school's WBGT meter for about an hour on **Oct 17 or 18**? We'd set it up in the sun 3 feet off the field the way Policy 41.7 says, record a reading every 15 minutes, and compare with the app. We'll share the results with you.

Thank you,
[Name]

What to record (for the side-by-side test): time, meter WBGT, app WBGT, sun/cloud, wind feel, and a photo of the meter screen each time. At least 4 readings, ideally across noon. The model has been seen reading high when the forecast wind is near calm, so note when the air is still.
